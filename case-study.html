PROJECT 01 • PROJECT STORY

Footstep Power
Harvesting System

The prototype demonstrated the feasibility of converting mechanical energy from human footsteps into electrical energy and storing the harvested energy for practical low-power applications.

01 // The Problem

Footsteps generate mechanical energy that is normally wasted in our daily environments. The objective of this project was to capture this ambient mechanical energy and convert it into usable electrical energy using piezoelectric transducers.

02 // Physical Prototype

The 40 piezoelectric discs were physically arranged beneath a stepping platform and connected to the power-conditioning and storage circuitry. This was a fully functional real hardware prototype, designed to validate the concept in a practical scenario.

06 // Engineering Challenge

Piezoelectric elements produce relatively small and variable electrical outputs. The primary engineering challenge was designing proper rectification, voltage conditioning, and efficient energy storage to stabilize this output before it could be practically demonstrated.
